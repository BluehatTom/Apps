Quick start
Choose a shape, then click and drag on the canvas. Select a shape to edit its exact measurements, or click a blue dimension value to change its size. Drag the shape to reposition it. Use Measure to draw a distance between two points. Change the selected object’s background color in Properties. Use Save to computer for an editable local file.

Export your design

Uses actual measurements in mm or inches. Exports include visible labels and dimensions unless otherwise noted.
The outlines-only option applies to SVG, DXF, PNG and print/PDF. STL only contains closed shapes (rectangles, circles, etc.) with their specified extrusion depths; text, dimension marks, open lines and measurement annotations are not embossed. Overlapping shapes are not unioned. Use a slicer to inspect printed parts.

3D extrusion preview

Drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Extrusion depth is editable per shape in the Properties panel. This previews printable geometry, not 3D lettering.
