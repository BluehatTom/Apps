Quick start
Choose a shape, then click and drag on the canvas. Select a shape to edit its exact measurements, or click a blue dimension value to change its size. Drag the shape to reposition it. Drag empty canvas, use the Hand tool, or hold Space to pan. Select a solid shape and choose Screw hole to position an M3/M4/etc. tapped hole. Use Measure to draw a distance between two points. Change the selected object’s background color in Properties. Use Save to computer for an editable local file.

Export your design

Uses actual measurements in mm or inches. Exports include visible labels and dimensions unless otherwise noted.
The outlines-only option applies to SVG, DXF, PNG and print/PDF. STL includes only shapes marked as 3D solids, with hole cavities cut out. Modeled internal threads are a 60°-profile-inspired printable approximation of metric coarse threads, not manufacturing-certified threads. Check fit, extrusion tolerances, and slicer repair warnings. Overlapping separate solids are not unioned; labels and dimensions do not become 3D geometry.

3D extrusion preview

Drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Extrusion depth, solid status, and holes are editable in Properties. The preview uses the same solid and hole geometry as STL. Text is not embossed.

Screw hole

A hole cuts into the selected 3D solid at the position clicked. Modeled thread creates an actual helical cavity in the STL; Tap-drill makes an untapped hole intended for subsequent tapping.
Metric coarse pitch; modeled 60°-style helical threads are an approximation for FDM prototyping, not a certified ISO profile. Verify dimensions and test fit before using in a load-bearing assembly.
