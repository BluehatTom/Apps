What changed in V5.4?

Fit and blueprint update: independent lid and mechanical bore fit presets, corrected sliding-lid tracks and shaped-wall offsets, printed shaft/crank hardware clearances, gear tooth phasing, and dimensioned blueprint/specification exports. Enclosure assemblies and solid gear-kit hardware are checked for component overlap before export. Regenerate older saved designs to use these corrections.

Quick Start is now the first main-menu item and is present on every tool page. Tool pages filter presets to their own designs, preventing stale cross-tool selections.

Compound Gear Lab presets can now generate printable low-load shafts, base/inter-stage spacers, retainer caps, and a real hand-crank arm/handle where requested.

Gears & Drives is now a Gear Lab with application presets, multi-stage compound trains, auto-solving toward a target ratio, planetary sets, rack-and-pinion applications, kinetic/exposed gear layouts, live motion preview, and artistic face treatments.

Gear geometry still uses module, pressure angle, backlash, and independent bore allowances. External involute gears use a pressure-angle-aware minimum because this lightweight generator does not model undercut/profile-shift geometry: 14.5° starts at 32T, 20° at 18T, and 25° at 12T.

Enclosures retain the expanded V5 shape library and the more conservative hinge-size guidance.

The old 65 mm width / 22 mm wall hinge rule was geometry-only. V5 uses a larger empirical starting recommendation, scales it with wall and pin size, and distinguishes a recommended print envelope from the absolute geometry floor.

Wall fit remains independent from hinge clearance. The snug preset starts at 0.08 mm total wall-joint clearance, and the successful hinge clearance is unchanged.

The packer still uses conservative rectangle envelopes with 90° rotations; slicer review remains required.

Limits & source notes

The V3/V4 hinged enclosure was physically test-printed by the user and assembled successfully; V5 keeps that hinge clearance but uses more conservative size guidance. The newer enclosure shapes have not all been physically validated. Mechanical prototypes need a small fit test. Mesh checks do not certify strength, dimensional accuracy, fatigue, every assembly path, or every self-intersection.

There is no automatic support generation or print-time calculation. Material selection produces guidance, not automatic shrinkage compensation.

Printer presets: Creality K2 SE and Bambu A1 mini. Compare the usable area with your slicer, including clips, purge lines and exclusion zones.

Prusa: design, fit and support considerations. The 45° face test here is deliberately conservative, not a universal printing limit.
