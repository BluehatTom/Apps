    Notes and checks

    1. Locating tabs with matching open-edge sockets. These locate the panels; use adhesive for a permanent box. A dry friction fit is not a positive lock.
    1. [pass] Assembled solid interference check: Separate modeled solids have no positive-volume overlap above 0.01 mm³ in their assembled position. Surface contact is allowed. This checks solid components at the assembled position, not every insertion path or hinge angle; direct-mesh gear teeth use their nominal pitch geometry.
    2. [info] Independent mating allowances: Wall-joint clearance 0.08 mm total; lid clearance 0.3 mm total; other selected fits 0.3 mm total. Hinge moving clearance remains 0.35 mm.
    3. [pass] Closed, consistently wound meshes: 6 parts passed edge, winding, positive-volume and expected-shell checks. This does not prove absence of self-intersection.
    4. [pass] Every part fits the selected build volume: 2 print plates, with 4 mm edge margin, 5 mm minimum part gap, and 0 mm brim allowance per side.
    5. [pass] Nominal wall/nozzle ratio: 7.5 nozzle widths across the nominal wall. Local grooves and flexures are evaluated separately where known.
    6. [pass] Explicit fit clearance: 0.08 mm TOTAL fit clearance is used for the relevant generated joints (0.04 mm nominal per side on symmetric slots). Hinge moving gaps remain independent. These are starting values, not calibrated material guarantees.
    7. [pass] No significant steep downward faces: No more than 2 mm2 of steep downward faces per part in the chosen print orientation. Internal bridges and toolpaths still require slicer review.
    8. [info] What this check does not certify: No slicer simulation, automatic support design, exhaustive collision/self-intersection testing, minimum-wall scan of arbitrary meshes, thermal analysis, print-time estimate or strength calculation. Always inspect the STL in your slicer.
