A quick start

  1. Import videos or images, then click them to add clips.
  2. Arrange your sequence by dragging clips or using Move.
  3. Edit a selected clip: trim, split, adjust speed or volume, and add text.
  4. Transition from the selected clip to the next with a dissolve, fade, or wipe.
  5. Export your video. Export runs in real time; keep this tab visible until it finishes.
